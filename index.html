The Hidden Problem

Oil fouling is costing you thousands — silently.

Every refrigeration and HVAC system accumulates oil fouling over time. This invisible layer acts as an insulator, forcing your compressors to work harder and burn more energy.

20–30%

Efficiency loss attributable to oil fouling alone — per ASHRAE research. On a $10,000/month cooling bill, that's up to $3,000 wasted every single month.

The Solution

How Cold-Plus® Works

A four-step process that restores your systems to peak performance without shutdowns or equipment replacement.

1

Remove Oil Fouling

Cold-Plus® circulates through the system, binding to and removing oil deposits from heat exchanger surfaces.

2

Improve Heat Transfer

With fouling removed, refrigerant contacts clean metal surfaces — dramatically improving thermal conductivity.

3

Enhance Thermodynamics

Lower head pressures and reduced compression ratios mean the system runs cooler and more efficiently.

4

Reduce Friction & Wear

Lubricity additives protect compressor components, extending equipment life and reducing maintenance costs.
