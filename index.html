The Solution

How Cold-Plus® works

A single treatment. No shutdowns. No equipment changes. Cold-Plus® works inside your existing system.

1

Removes Oil Fouling

Surfactants and detergents dissolve the oil film coating your heat-exchanger surfaces. This is the same film that has been silently dragging down your efficiency since day one.

2

Improves Pool Boiling

Polymers enhance the refrigerant's phase-change process up to 20% faster, dramatically improving the rate at which heat is absorbed and transferred out of your system.

3

Speeds Thermodynamics

Nano-polymers reduce surface tension in the refrigerant, allowing faster evaporation and condensation cycles. The system starts moving heat the way it was designed to.

4

Reduces Friction

A molecular coating decreases compressor workload and wear. You get lower head pressures, reduced amp draw, quieter operation, and significantly extended compressor life.
